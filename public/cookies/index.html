Cookie policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This site doesn't use cookies. That's why there's no cookie banner: there's nothing to ask your consent for.

What this site stores on your device

  • No cookies — none of its own and none from anyone else.
  • No other storage — no local storage, session storage or anything similar.
  • Visitor statistics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and stores nothing on your device. The privacy policy describes what it records.

What your browser may keep anyway

Like any website, your browser may keep ordinary cached copies of pages, styles, fonts and images so the site loads faster next time. That's your browser's cache, not a cookie, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Cloudflare security checks

If Cloudflare ever suspects a visit is automated traffic, it may show that visitor a security check and set a short-lived cookie (cf_clearance) remembering that the check was passed. It's strictly necessary for security, is used only for that, and I never receive it. The site sets no such cookie in normal use.

Other sites

Sending the Thrennel waitlist form doesn't set any cookie. If you follow a link to thrennel.com, that site has its own privacy policy and cookie policy.

If this changes

If this site ever starts using cookies or similar technologies that need your consent, this page will be updated first, and you'll be asked before any of them are set.
